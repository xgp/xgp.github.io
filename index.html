By ten, the old town was full of them, holding phones in front of the painted facades, queuing for a nineteen-euro Aperol Spritz, buying lavender sachets stitched in a factory nowhere near Provence. By four they were gone, and the quay was hosed down for the next ship.

And because of that, the reasoning goes, the people who build it stand on higher ground than the people who build Elasticsearch, or Redis, or Terraform. The license is not just a legal instrument.
